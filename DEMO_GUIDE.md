# Interactive Demo Guide - MobiFin

This guide provides a step-by-step presentation script to demonstrate MobiFin's core capabilities (SEE → PREDICT → ACT → ACCESS) during a live hackathon pitch or testing session.

---

## Preparation: Resetting the Environment
Before starting your walk-through, initialize the database to make sure the Kwame Centre and Customer #1048 narrative is loaded:
1. Open the application.
2. Click the **"Reset Demo Data"** button at the bottom of the sidebar. 
3. Verify that the database indicator in the top header displays your active connection (either PostgreSQL or SQLite Fallback).

---

## Narrative Phase 1: The Agent Experience (SEE → PREDICT → ACT)
*Goal: Demonstrate how an agent manages daily liquidity and avoids float stockouts.*

### Step 1: Sign in as the Agent
1. On the login screen, click the **"Agent (Kwame)"** quick-access button.
2. The application logs you in as Kwame and opens the **Dashboard**.

### Step 2: SEE (Observe the Business Health)
1. Point out the KPI cards:
   - **Cash on Hand:** `GH₵4,850.00`
   - **E-Float Balance:** `GH₵7,200.00`
   - **Agent Business Health Score:** `87/100` (highlight the teal circular gauge indicating stable operations).
2. Point out the **AI Insights Engine** on the right side of the dashboard, showing grounded insights: *"Daily commission inflows are highly consistent and predictable."*

### Step 3: PREDICT (Demand Forecasting)
1. Navigate to the **Liquidity Demand** tab on the sidebar.
2. Point out the **Expected E-Float Demand** of `GH₵11,400.00` predicted for tomorrow.
3. Observe the **Projected Shortfall** card showing `GH₵4,200.00` (since current float is only `GH₵7,200.00`).
4. Show the **Demand vs Holdings Forecast** bar chart comparing the predicted volume surge against Kwame's current float limit.

### Step 4: ACT (Actionable Rebalancing)
1. Focus on the **Calculated Rebalancing Script** card:
   - The recommendation engine deterministically suggests: **Rebalance GH₵4,000 before 10:30 AM** from Cash into E-Float to prevent transaction failure.
2. Point out the **Liquidity Stress-Test Simulator** on the right, showing how demand surges of +10%, +20%, and +30% spike the shortfall and elevate operational risk levels.
3. Navigate to **Business Analytics** to show volume trends and regional operational traffic.

## Narrative Phase 2: Underbanked Credit Assessment (ACCESS)
*Goal: Demonstrate how regular transactions serve as alternative financial data to unlock credit.*

### Step 5: Switch Role to the Financial Institution
1. In the header's **Demo Mode View** selector, click the **"FI (Forms Capital)"** button.
2. The UI automatically transitions, loading the institutional view and opening **Customer Intelligence**.

### Step 6: Search the Matured Consented Customer (#1048)
1. In the left customer list, select **Customer #1048** (or type `1048` in search).
2. Point out that this customer is unbanked and has no conventional credit record, but has signed the opt-in profiling form (**Consent: ACTIVE**).
3. Observe their **Alternative Credit Score** generated by the XGBoost model:
   - **Alternative Credit Score:** `764 / 850` (Low Risk)
   - **Repayment Probability:** `91.0%`
   - **Indicative Credit Capacity:** `GH₵7,500.00` (Note: Highlight the warning disclaimer that final underwriting lies with the lending institution).
4. Direct attention to the card titled **"Factors influencing this assessment"** (SHAP values):
   - Highlight that the score is explainable. Positive features (e.g., *Savings Behavior Score, Inflow Outflow Ratio*) pushed the score up, while negative features (e.g., *Cashflow Volatility*) adjusted it downwards.
5. Review the **Observed Inflow vs Outflow History** bar chart showing a steady cash flow trend.

### Step 7: Demonstrate Gated Progress (Insufficient History / No Consent)
1. Select another customer from the list who lacks consent or has short history (e.g., a newly created customer).
2. Point out that the system **refuses to fabricate a credit score**.
3. Instead, the UI displays a **Financial Readiness Score** progress board (0-100) indicating how close the customer is to maturity (e.g. `23/30` transactions, `67/90` days).
4. Point out the **Consent Manager** page on the sidebar, showing how agents can help customers toggle consent states.

## Narrative Phase 3: Model and Data Auditing
*Goal: Show the technical validity of the platform.*

### Step 8: Switch Role to Admin
1. In the header, click the **"Admin"** button.
2. Navigate to **Model Performance** to display the actual, non-hardcoded XGBoost validation ROC-AUC, F1, MAE, and Isolation Forest contamination scores.
3. Navigate to **Data Explorer** to view the overall database counts, consenting profile ratios, and database types.
